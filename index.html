NZ Education Participation & Outcomes Insights

Analytical approach

Work that holds up beyond the chart.

My priority is not just a polished visualization. It is connecting stakeholder questions, reliable data and decisions while being transparent about uncertainty.

01

Frame the question

Translate business or policy objectives into clear metrics, appropriate comparisons and a manageable scope.

02

Validate the data

Check keys, denominators, source coverage, missingness, consistency and the limits of what the data represents.

03

Make insight actionable

Compare segments and trends, distinguish evidence from hypotheses and recommend practical follow-up analysis.
